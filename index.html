      How to play

      Sink all five enemy ships before they sink yours.

      1. Deploy your fleet. Choose an opponent and map. Shuffle your ships until you like their formation, then deploy. Your ships occupy 5, 4, 3, 3, and 2 cells.
      2. Fire by coordinate. Select an unknown coordinate on the enemy board. A cross (×) marks a hit; a dot (•) marks a miss. Islands cannot be targeted.
      3. Fire again on a hit. Every hit earns another shot. A miss passes the turn to your opponent.

      In pass & play, hand the device to the named player before revealing their board. The probability lens estimates ship positions from known shots; it never reveals hidden ships.
